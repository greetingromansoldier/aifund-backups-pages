AIFund Backups

A small utility for storing encrypted backup archives in Google Drive.

AIFund Backups is an operator-managed backup workflow built around rclone. Authorized AIFund operators use it to upload, verify, retrieve, and manage backup archives in an AIFund-controlled Google Drive account.

Information only. This website contains no backups, credentials, or access to the trading platform. It is not a trading service and has no public sign-up.

Why it needs Google Drive

Google Drive provides storage outside the platform's own server. The account owner authorizes the utility through Google's OAuth sign-in process; the utility does not ask for the account password.

The requested permission is drive.file. It is used to manage this application's backup files and folders, rather than request full access to the account's other Drive files.

Encryption and recovery

The required backup procedure encrypts archives before upload and keeps the private decryption key separately from the backup host. Restoring an archive requires that key. Google Drive storage does not replace integrity checks or a restore test.

Backup schedules and retention are configured separately by the operators. This information page does not confirm that an automated backup job is running.
